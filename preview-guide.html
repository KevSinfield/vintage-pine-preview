What’s in this preview

A redesign preview for Vintage Pine Etc. Ltd. It is not the official shop (that is vintagepineetc.co.uk) and is not an importable Divi layout. It shows how the approved design would be rebuilt in the existing WordPress, Divi and WooCommerce setup.

In scope

  • Homepage: full design.
  • Item page, one shared layout: available item (Puritan Soap sign) and sold item (pine dresser, c.1890).
  • Something to sell?: the route for people offering an item.

Simplified stand-ins (outside the demo scope)

  • Collection pages (for example Furniture) show only the sample items, so the links work.
  • Contact uses the live page’s wording with an optional form added.
  • Basket, checkout, account, search and pagination are not built.

Simulated

  • “Add to basket” shows a demo message. Nothing is added, ordered or paid for.
  • The Sell and Contact forms do not send anything. Selected photos stay in the browser.
  • All listings come from one sample data file (data/catalogue.json): 15 real listings copied from the public shop on 12 Nov 2026, with their own text, prices and photos.
