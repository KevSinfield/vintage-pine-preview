What’s in this preview

A redesign preview for Vintage Pine Etc. Ltd. It is not the official shop (that is vintagepineetc.co.uk) and is not an importable Divi layout. It shows how the approved design would be rebuilt in the existing WordPress, Divi and WooCommerce setup.

Finished in this preview

  • Homepage: full design.
  • Item page, one shared layout: available item (Puritan Soap sign) and sold item (pine dresser, c.1890).
  • Something to sell?: the route for people offering an item.

Still to come

These pages are rough stand-ins so the links work. They are not the finished design yet.

  • Collection pages (for example Furniture) show only the sample items, so the links work. See About the collection pages.
  • Contact uses the live page’s wording with an optional form added.
  • Basket, checkout, account, search and pagination are not built.

Simulated

  • “Add to basket” shows a demo message. Nothing is added, ordered or paid for.
  • The Sell and Contact forms do not send anything. Selected photos stay in the browser.
  • Visit notes: when this preview is opened or used, it sends the designer a short anonymous note saying which page, the type of device and the screen width. There are no cookies or visitor IDs, nothing typed into a form is included, and no IP addresses are logged. It is for the preview only and won’t be on the real shop.
  • All listings come from one sample data file (data/catalogue.json): 15 real listings copied from the public shop on 12 Nov 2026, with their own text, prices and photos.

About the collection pages

Each collection page is a simple stand-in so the menu and category links work. It lists only the items in the preview’s sample data, not the full category. In production it is the WooCommerce product category archive, built as a Divi Theme Builder “Product category” template, which keeps the shop’s existing pagination and Sold badges.

Card titles on listings are shortened for display (for example “Puritan Soap enamel sign”). The full product name stays on the item page; in WooCommerce that would be a short display title or simply the product name, the client’s choice.
