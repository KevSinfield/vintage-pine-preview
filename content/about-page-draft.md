This is the shop's own welcome text, as used on the preview homepage before the shorter version replaced it (12 Nov 2026). It's saved here for a future About page. It's lightly tidied from the live homepage: "lays" is changed to "lies" and the punctuation is tidied.

## Pine, oak and curious finds.

Our aim is to provide a constantly changing variety of carefully selected Victorian, Edwardian and other vintage furniture, all of which have that timeless charm that pieces of a certain style and quality retain.

But there is more, much more. Hidden behind the *etc.* part of our name lies a world of interesting, quirky and occasionally wacky items, ranging from industrial heritage products to former stage props, together with a totally random collection of odds and ends.
